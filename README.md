Twitter / X Social Media Analytics

An end-to-end Data Analytics and Power BI dashboard project focused
on analyzing Twitter/X content performance, audience reach, engagement,
interaction behavior, and time-based performance.

The project combines data cleaning and exploratory analysis with an
interactive four-page Power BI dashboard.

📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to convert tweet-level social media
data into actionable business insights around:

Content volume

Audience reach

Engagement volume

Engagement efficiency

Interaction types

Top-performing tweets

Monthly performance

Day-of-week performance

Time-based performance

The analysis was performed using the supplied Twitter/X analytical
dataset, while Power BI, Power Query, and DAX
were used to build the final interactive dashboard.

🎯 Project Objectives

Measure overall Twitter/X publishing activity.

Analyze impressions and audience reach.

Measure total engagements and engagement rate.

Understand the contribution of individual interaction types.

Identify high-performing tweets.

Compare performance across months, weeks, days, and time periods.

Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business-oriented social
media analysis.

🔎 Exploratory Analysis

Impression Distribution

Most tweets fall within the lower impression ranges, while a small
number of tweets generate exceptionally high reach. This produces a
strong difference between typical tweet performance and the maximum
observed performance.

The highest observed impression value is 156,685.

Engagement Distribution

Engagement is also concentrated toward lower values. The median
engagement is much lower than the maximum of 54,098, showing that a
small number of high-performing tweets contribute disproportionately to
total engagement.

Engagement Rate

Engagement rate is analyzed separately from raw engagement volume so
that high-volume tweets and high-efficiency tweets can be distinguished.

📈 Statistical Analysis

The analytical workflow also examines relationships between impressions,
engagements and engagement rate.

The notebook analysis shows a strong positive association between
impressions and engagements, while engagement rate behaves
differently from raw reach volume.

This distinction is important because a tweet can have high reach
without necessarily having the highest engagement efficiency.

Correlation describes association in the dataset; it does not
establish causation.

💡 Key Business Insights

1. Reach is concentrated

Most tweets generate relatively modest reach, while a small number of
tweets produce exceptionally high impressions.

2. Engagement is concentrated

The maximum engagement value is substantially higher than typical
tweet-level engagement, showing the impact of high-performing content.

3. Friday is a major high-volume day

Friday records approximately 270K impressions and 60K engagements,
making it the highest visible day for total performance volume.

4. July is a major monthly performance period

July records approximately 345K impressions in the Power BI
dashboard and is the strongest month by total reach in the analysis.

5. Reach and efficiency should be evaluated separately

Total impressions measure visibility, while engagement rate measures
efficiency. Both provide different information about content
performance.

6. Interaction types represent different audience behaviors

Likes, clicks, replies and retweets should not be treated as identical
actions. Separating them provides a more useful view of audience
response.

💼 Business Recommendations

Analyze high-performing content

Investigate the characteristics of tweets that generate exceptionally
high impressions or engagement and use those findings for future content
experiments.

Monitor reach and engagement together

Use impressions, total engagements and engagement rate together instead
of relying on a single KPI.

Evaluate posting-time patterns

Use day-of-week and time analysis to test publishing schedules and
compare performance across time periods.

Track interaction types separately

Monitor likes, clicks, replies and retweets independently to understand
different audience behaviors.

Use interactive dashboard filtering

Use the Power BI date, month, quarter, week and day-of-week controls to
investigate specific periods and identify changes in performance.

These recommendations are based on observed patterns in the project
data and should be treated as areas for further testing rather than
causal conclusions.
